Privacy Policy for SAFE Easy Privacy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy is a draft for the Safe re-release. It is not legal advice. It describes the local-only product we intend to ship. It applies only if the live app matches this description.

Who we are

SAFE Easy Privacy ("Safe") is an iPhone app published by Rodney Gainous Jr. Safe is a local PGP tool. You use it to create a key, lock and unlock messages, share your public key, and back up your keys on this device.

Data we do not collect

Safe does not collect personal information from the app.

We do not have an account system. We do not run servers for Safe. We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporters, or tracking SDKs in Safe. We do not sell your data.

Apple's App Store privacy label for this app should be "Data Not Collected" only when the live build matches this policy.

What stays on your device

Your keys, passphrases, locked and unlocked messages, and backups that you keep in Safe stay on your device. Safe does not send them to us.

A passphrase you choose is used on your device to protect your key. We cannot see it and we cannot reset it for you.

If Safe offers Face ID or Touch ID as a convenience lock, that check stays on your device.

Sharing you choose to do

When you share a public key, a locked message, or a backup file, you choose where it goes (for example Messages, Mail, or the Files app). Safe does not send us a copy.

If you save a file to iCloud Drive, another cloud folder, or another app, that service has its own rules. Safe does not receive that file from those services.

Optional Safe Pro purchase

Safe's core tools are free. Optional Safe Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your card number or Apple Account password. Apple's privacy policy covers that checkout.

Children's privacy

Safe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated text at this URL and update the effective date.
