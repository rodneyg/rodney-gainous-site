Support for SAFE Easy Privacy

SAFE Easy Privacy is a local PGP app for iPhone. Your keys stay on your device. There is no account and no Safe server.

Get started

  1. Open Safe and create a key with a passphrase you choose.
  2. Share or copy your public key with the person you want to write to.
  3. Lock a message to their public key, or unlock a message sent to you.
  4. Back up your keys and keep that backup somewhere only you control.
  5. In Messages, tap plus, pick Safe, then share your public key, lock a message, or unlock one you received.

What we cannot do

We cannot see your keys, messages, or passphrase. We cannot reset a forgotten passphrase. If you lose your passphrase and you do not have a backup, we cannot recover the key.
